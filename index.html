About Our Laboratories

Chemistry Laboratory

A chemistry laboratory is a specialized workspace equipped with scientific instruments and chemical reagents. It is used to conduct controlled experiments, analyze substances, and research chemical properties and reactions.

Physics Laboratory

A physics laboratory is a dedicated workspace for practical experimentation, observation, and measurement of physical phenomena. It allows students, researchers, and scientists to test theories on matter, energy, forces, and motion in a controlled environment.

Biology Laboratory

A biology laboratory is a specialized workspace equipped with scientific instruments where biological research, experiments, and analyses are conducted. It bridges theoretical life science concepts with practical applications, enabling study of living organisms and cellular processes.
